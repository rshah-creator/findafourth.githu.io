Find a Fourth

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Find a Fourth ("we", "our", or "us") is a golf matchmaking app that connects golfers looking for playing partners. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account or setting up your profile:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • City and home golf course
  • Profile photo
  • Golf handicap, preferred tees, playing pace, and availability schedule
  • Game broadcasts you post and games you join

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display your profile to other golfers in your city
  • Match you with compatible playing partners
  • Send notifications about games you've joined or broadcast
  • Keep your account secure and verify your identity

Information Visible to Other Users

Your first name, city, handicap, profile photo, home course, and playing preferences are visible to other Find a Fourth users. Your email address and phone number are never shown to other users.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except as needed to operate the service (see Third-Party Services below) or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

Find a Fourth uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage
  • Expo — app delivery and push notifications
  • Infobip — SMS delivery for phone verification

Push Notifications

If you grant permission, we send push notifications about game activity (players joining or leaving, cancellations, and round reminders). You can disable notifications at any time in the app or your device settings.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Find a Fourth is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
