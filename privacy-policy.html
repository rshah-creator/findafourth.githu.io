Find a Fourth

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Find a Fourth ("we", "our", or "us") is a golf matchmaking app that connects golfers looking for playing partners. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account or setting up your profile:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Date of birth — collected once during sign-up solely to verify you're old enough to use the app; it's never shown to other users
  • City and home golf course
  • Profile photo
  • Golf handicap, preferred tees, playing pace, availability schedule, and whether you're open to playing rounds with side betting (a personal preference tag only — see "Betting" below)
  • Game broadcasts you post and games you join
  • Push notification token and notification preferences, if you grant notification permission

Device Contacts (Optional)

If you grant contacts permission, the app sends your device contacts' phone numbers to our server to check which of them are already Find a Fourth members, so we can suggest them as friends. Your contacts are only used for this matching check — we don't store your full contact list, and we never show your contacts to other users. Only matches (and the limited profile info of those matched members) are returned to your device.

Betting

The "betting" field on your profile and on broadcasts is a preference/matching tag only — it indicates whether a player is open to friendly side bets during a round. Find a Fourth does not process, hold, or facilitate any money changing hands between players. Any side bets players arrange are strictly between those players.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Display your profile to other golfers in your city
  • Match you with compatible playing partners
  • Suggest friends from your contacts, if you grant that permission
  • Send notifications about games you've joined or broadcast
  • Keep your account secure and verify your identity

Information Visible to Other Users

Your first name, city, handicap, profile photo, home course, and playing preferences are visible to other Find a Fourth users. Your email address, phone number, and date of birth are never shown to other users.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except as needed to operate the service (see Third-Party Services below) or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

Find a Fourth uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage
  • Expo — app delivery and push notifications
  • Infobip — SMS delivery for phone verification

Push Notifications

If you grant permission, we send push notifications about game activity (players joining or leaving, cancellations, and round reminders). We store a push token and your notification preferences to deliver these and let you control which ones you receive. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or your device settings.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request account deletion at any time from Settings. Requesting deletion signs you out immediately and starts a 14-day grace period — during that window, simply signing back in cancels the deletion and restores your account. If you don't sign back in within 14 days, your account and associated data (profile, messages, broadcasts, friend requests, and uploaded photos) are permanently deleted.

Children's Privacy

Find a Fourth is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
